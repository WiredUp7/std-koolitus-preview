See dokument selgitab, milliseid andmeid little big heart rakenduse ja karbi kasutamisel kogutakse, miks, kui kaua neid hoitakse ja millised õigused Sul oma andmete suhtes on. Kehtib nii veebirakendusele kui ka Google Plays avaldatud Androidi rakendusele.

little big heart on sõnumikarp täiskasvanutele ja peredele. See ei ole turva-, valve- ega jälgimisseade. Kõik siin kirjeldatud andmed on vajalikud selleks, et Sinu sõnum ja pilt jõuaksid karpi ning et Sa saaksid teada, kas see jõudis kohale.

2. Milliseid andmeid töötleme

2.1 Konto (liitud kutselingiga)

  • Nimi, mille ise liitumisel sisestad või hiljem seadetes muudad.
  • Profiilipilt, kui selle ise lisad (valikuline). Pilt salvestatakse ruudukujulise JPEG-failina ilma algse foto lisaandmeteta (nt EXIF). Pilti näidatakse karbi ekraanil Sinu nime kõrval.
  • Sisselogimistoken: juhuslik pikk tunnus, mis toimib parooli asemel. Serveris hoitakse ainult selle räsi, mitte tunnust ennast.
  • Millistesse karpidesse kuulud ja mis rollis (omanik või saatja), liitumise aeg ja täisealisuse kinnituse aeg.
  • Rakenduse viimase kasutamise aeg.

2.2 Sõnumid (saatjana)

  • Sõnumi tekst (kuni 240 märki).
  • Pilt või kuni 15-sekundiline video, kui selle lisad. Pilt vähendatakse ja salvestatakse JPEG-ina ilma algse faili lisaandmeteta. Video teisendatakse karbi jaoks sobivaks failiks.
  • Südame värv, saatmise aeg ja ajastatud sõnumi puhul soovitud kohalejõudmise aeg.
  • Sündmused Sinu sõnumi kohta: millal sõnum loeti, kas karbi juurest saadeti süda tagasi ja kas sõnum märgiti lemmikuks.

2.3 Karbi omanikuna

  • Kõik punktis 2.2 nimetatu kõigi Sinu karpi saadetud sõnumite kohta.
  • Karbi liikmete nimekiri (nimed, rollid, liitumise aeg) ja Sinu loodud kutselingid.
  • Karbi tehniline olek, et näeksid, kas karp on võrgus.

2.4 Mida karp ise serverile saadab

Karp küsib serverilt uusi sõnumeid umbes iga 10 sekundi järel ja saadab umbes iga 10 minuti järel tehnilise olekuteate: püsivara versioon, tööaeg, WiFi signaali tugevus, viimase taaskäivituse põhjus, vaba mälu, mälukaardi olek ning sõnumite ja lemmikute arv kaardil (arvuna, mitte sisuna) ja lühikesed tehnilised logiread. Karp ei saada kaane avamise ega puudutuste andmeid.

2.5 Teavitused

Kui lubad teavitused, salvestame Sinu seadme teavitustellimuse aadressi, et saaksime Sulle teada anda, kui sõnum loeti, süda saadeti tagasi või sõnum sai lemmikuks.

2.6 Serveri logi

Server salvestab iga päringu kohta IP-aadressi, aja ja aadressi. Seda kasutame turvalisuse ja tõrgete uurimiseks ning liigsete päringute tõkestamiseks.

2.7 Mida me ei kogu

Me ei küsi e-posti aadressi ega telefoninumbrit. Me ei kogu asukohta ega kontakte. Rakenduses ei ole analüütika- ega reklaamikoodi. Me ei müü ega jaga Sinu andmeid turunduseks.

Privacy policy

Effective from 21 October 2026

This policy explains what data is collected when you use the little big heart app and box, why, how long it is kept and what rights you have over your data. It applies to the web app and to the Android app published on Google Play.

little big heart is a message box for adults and families. It is not a security, surveillance or tracking device. All data described here is needed so that your message and photo reach the box and so that you can see whether it arrived.

2. What data we process

2.1 Account (joining with an invite link)

  • The name you enter when joining or change later in settings.
  • A profile picture, if you add one (optional). It is stored as a square JPEG without the original photo's metadata (such as EXIF) and shown on the box screen next to your name.
  • A sign-in token: a long random code that works instead of a password. The server only keeps a hash of it, not the code itself.
  • Which boxes you belong to and in what role (owner or sender), when you joined and when you confirmed that you are an adult.
  • When you last used the app.

2.2 Messages (as a sender)

  • The message text (up to 240 characters).
  • A photo or a video of up to 15 seconds, if you add one. Photos are resized and saved as JPEG without the original file's metadata. Videos are converted into a format the box can play.
  • The heart colour, the time of sending and, for scheduled messages, the chosen delivery time.
  • Events about your message: when it was read, whether a heart was sent back from the box and whether it was marked as a favourite.

2.3 As a box owner

  • Everything listed in 2.2 for all messages sent to your box.
  • The list of box members (names, roles, joining time) and the invite links you have created.
  • The technical status of the box, so you can see whether it is online.

2.4 What the box sends to the server

The box checks for new messages about every 10 seconds and sends a technical status report about every 10 minutes: firmware version, uptime, Wi-Fi signal strength, the reason for the last restart, free memory, memory card status, the number of messages and favourites on the card (as a count, not content) and short technical log lines. The box does not send data about opening the lid or touches.

2.5 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's notification subscription address so that we can tell you when a message was read, a heart was sent back or a message became a favourite.

2.6 Server log

For each request the server records the IP address, the time and the address requested. We use this for security, troubleshooting and limiting excessive requests.

2.7 What we do not collect

We do not ask for your email address or phone number. We do not collect location or contacts. The app contains no analytics or advertising code. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

4. Who receives data

  • The box owner and the people near the box see the messages sent to it. That is what the service is for.
  • The server is located in Estonia and run by us. The connection to the server goes through Tailscale Inc., which forwards the encrypted connection without seeing its content. Tailscale sees technical connection data such as the IP address and time.
  • Notifications are delivered by your device's or browser's push service (Google on Android and in Chrome, Apple on Apple devices, Mozilla in Firefox, Microsoft in Edge). The notification content is encrypted; the service sees the time of sending and the target device.

These providers may process data outside the European Union. If you do not allow notifications, nothing is sent to push services and the rest of the app works the same.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, restrict its processing, receive it in a machine-readable format and object to processing (GDPR Art. 15-21). We reply within one month.

In the app: Settings → Your account

  • "Download my data" gives you a zip file with your profile and the messages you have sent.
  • "Delete my account" immediately deletes your account and all your data from the server. Details: account deletion.

You can also send a request to info@look360.eu.

If you are a box owner, deleting your account does not delete the box or other members' accounts. All data of a box is deleted when the box is reset to factory settings.

7. Complaints

If you think your data has been processed incorrectly, please write to us first. You also have the right to contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon): Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, info@aki.ee, www.aki.ee. If you live in another EU country, you can also contact that country's data protection authority.

8. Age limit

Accounts are for adults. When joining, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. We do not ask for a date of birth. Children may look at messages on the family box screen, but they do not create accounts or send us any data.

9. Data on your device and on the box

The app keeps your sign-in token and language choice in your device's browser storage (in the Android app, in Chrome's storage). They are removed when you sign out or clear the browser data. The box keeps recent messages and favourites on its memory card so they can be viewed in the gallery.

10. Security

The connection between the app, the box and the server is encrypted (https). Sign-in tokens, invite links and box keys are stored hashed or encrypted on the server. Messages can only be seen by box members who are entitled to see them. Excessive requests are rate-limited.

11. Changes

We will announce significant changes in the app. The current version is always on this page.
